### Quant Crash Course Creation

Please create a Quantitative Finance crash course for a senior-level software engineer. The engineer has 20 years of programming experience and proficient data science skills. Please formulate the course in a zero-to-hero bootcamp style, including a glossary for all jargon and key terms. Ensure that any and all algorithms and formulas are included in the glossary. Where applicable, include flowcharts to diagram processes, thinking, and decision-making. Every discipline should include a comprehensive mind map, outlining all concepts and their connections to one another.

This course favors the Python programming language. The tone of each class should be playfully productive. Please include analogies and silly mnemonics to aid in understanding and remembering complex concepts. Every course should be taught from a perspective of arming the engineer with the ability to train, test, and optimize trading models. The models will be used by autonomous AI agents tasked with trading in stock, crypto, and forex markets. Please include projects that 1) require combining concepts to achieve a goal, 2) are deployable, and 3) provide a real-world benefit.
